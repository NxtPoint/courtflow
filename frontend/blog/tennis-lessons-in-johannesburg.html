Tennis Lessons in Johannesburg

26 July 2026

Looking for tennis lessons in Johannesburg? At NextPoint Tennis in Killarney, you can learn from ATP-level coaches — book online in under a minute and pay as you go — no membership required. Whether you've never held a racket or you're chasing a ranking, there's a lesson here for you.

We coach every age and level, seven days a week, on eight courts including Gauteng's only clay court — at Killarney Country Club in Houghton, a short drive from Rosebank, Parktown, Saxonwold and Sandton.

Book a lesson in 30 seconds

1. Open the booking page and choose Book a lesson. 2. Pick your coach and see their live availability. 3. Choose a time and confirm — pay online, at the court, or on a monthly account.

New to NextPoint? Start a free week — 7 days of free court bookings, no commitment. Lessons and classes are pay-as-you-go.

Learn from ATP-level coaches

Our coaching team brings professional-tour experience — including an ATP Coach of the Year — to club players, juniors and complete beginners right here in Johannesburg. Meet the coaches.

A lesson for every player

  • Private lessons — one-on-one, tailored to your goals. The fastest way to improve.
  • Semi-private (squad) lessons — share a coach with friends or family at a lower per-player rate.
  • Junior coaching — structured development for kids, from first racket to competitive squads.
  • Cardio Tennis & group classes — high-energy, social, and a great workout.
  • High-performance programme — for competitive players chasing rankings.

Beginners are genuinely welcome — rackets are provided, and every coach is used to starting players from zero.

Try the club for a week, free

There's no better way to see if we're the right fit. Start a free week — 7 days of court bookings on us, so you can play, look around and meet a coach before you commit to anything. Lessons and classes stay pay-as-you-go, with no membership required.

Where to find us

NextPoint Tennis, Killarney Country Club, Houghton, Johannesburg — close to Rosebank, Parktown and Sandton, with free on-site parking and floodlit courts for evening lessons after work.

Frequently asked questions

Do you offer tennis lessons for beginners? Yes — beginners are welcome, and rackets are provided.

Do you have tennis lessons for kids? Yes — structured junior coaching from first racket to competitive juniors.

Can I book a tennis lesson online? Yes — pick your coach and time in seconds at nextpointtennis.com.

How much are tennis lessons? You'll see live pricing when you book, and prepaid packs work out cheaper if you play often. See pricing.

Where are you located? Killarney Country Club, Houghton, Johannesburg — near Rosebank and Parktown.
