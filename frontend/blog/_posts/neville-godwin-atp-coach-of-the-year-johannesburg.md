Most club players will never hit with a coach who has worked a Grand Slam final. At **NextPoint Tennis** in Killarney, you can book one — by name, online, in about a minute.

Our Program Director, **Neville Godwin**, is the **2017 ATP Coach of the Year**. He coaches here, in Johannesburg, alongside a team with three decades of experience developing national champions and scholarship players.

## Who is Neville Godwin?

Neville's coaching career runs straight through the top of the professional game:

- **Kevin Anderson** — Neville coached him to the **world top 10** and the **2017 US Open final**. That run is what earned Neville the **ATP Coach of the Year** award.
- **Hyeon Chung** — guided to the **2018 Australian Open semi-finals**, the first South Korean player ever to reach a Grand Slam semi.
- **Reilly Opelka** — worked with him on tour.
- **Alexei Popyrin** — with Xavier Malisse, Neville helped develop Popyrin, including his **first ATP Masters 1000 title** (Montreal, 2024).

He has been coaching since 2004, and he is based here in Johannesburg.

His philosophy is deliberately broad: technical skill, tactical awareness, physical conditioning and mental toughness, with training built around the individual player — on and off the court.

## What that actually means for a club player

Tour-level credentials matter less as a trophy cabinet than as a diagnostic instinct. A coach who has prepared a player for a US Open final has seen every version of a breaking-down forehand, a second serve that leaks under pressure, and a game plan that survives the warm-up and dies in the third set.

You do not need to be chasing a ranking to benefit from that. The same eye that fixes a professional's return position fixes a club player's — usually faster, because the problem is more obvious.

## The rest of the team

Neville is one of two founders. **Ross Nemeth**, our Head Coach, has been coaching since 1993:

- Coached numerous **national junior champions** in singles and doubles, plus top ITF junior players.
- Guided one player from age 13 to the **U18 National Championship** and the **Junior French Open** — ending in a **full scholarship at a top-10 NCAA Division 1 college** in the USA.
- International experience at the Dutch National training facility and ITF events including the Junior Fed Cup (Barcelona), the Astrid Bowl (Belgium) and the Junior French Open.

Beyond the founders, the wider team covers every level and format:

- **Allon Rock** — 34 years coaching, 47 playing. Beginner to scholarship, plus cardio and boot-camp fitness.
- **Colbert Ndlovu** — 25+ years, TSA-certified. Player development from beginner to professional.
- **Terka Nambe** — 20+ years and a former ITF Seniors competitor. Technique, match confidence and high-performance juniors.

## Where we are

NextPoint Tennis is at **Killarney Country Club**, 60 5th Street, Houghton Estate — a short drive from Rosebank, Parktown, Saxonwold and Sandton.

There are **eight courts**: seven resurfaced hard courts, plus **the only clay court in Gauteng**. If you have never played on clay, it is worth the trip on its own — the ball sits up, the points get longer, and your footwork gets an education.
